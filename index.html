🗺️ Herbal Plants of India

🇮🇳

Herbal plants are traditionally grown and used across different regions of India.

South: Tulsi, Neem, Aloe Vera

North: Mint, Turmeric, Ginger

East: Hibiscus and various local herbs

West: Aloe Vera and medicinal herbs
